Portable Safes

Portable Safe Tips Unlike lockers and standard safes, Portable Safes don't have a fixed combination. The button sequence is randomised for each playthrough, so you'll need to solve the puzzle yourself. The aim is to light up all eight green indicators in order, moving either clockwise or anticlockwise around the circle. **It doesn't matter which light you start with**, as long as you follow the correct order without skipping any. Start by pressing different buttons to work out which light each one controls. Make a note of the correct sequence as you go. If you make a mistake, the puzzle resets, but the combination won't change. **Tip:** Take your time and write down the button order. Once you've worked out the sequence, you can enter it again without guessing. Each Portable Safe contains a Spare Key for the Safety Deposit Room keypad. Collecting both allows you to open locker 203 and grab an extra Hip Pouch.

Keys & Keycards

Important Keys

⚿ Spade Key

3F Stairwell

Found on a desk. Opens Spade doors throughout the Police Station.

⚿ Diamond Key

Morgue — Police Station B1

Found inside a body drawer. Opens Diamond doors.

⚿ Heart Key

Chief's Office — Private Collection Room

Examine the Relief Portrait to obtain the Heart Key. Exclusive to Claire.

⚿ Sewer Key

Bottom Waterway — Sewers

Found near the Bottom Waterway. Opens locked rooms in the Sewers.

Keycards

Weapons Locker Key Card

Art Room — 2F Police Station

Unlocks the weapons locker in the Safety Deposit Room, containing Claire's GM 79 Grenade Launcher.

Parking Garage Key Card

Chief's Office — Private Collection Room

Obtained after completing the electronic door panel puzzle. Unlocks the Parking Garage exit.

Other Keys

S.T.A.R.S. Badge / USB Key

Bejeweled Box

Combine the Red Jewel with the Bejeweled Box to obtain the badge. Examine it to reveal the USB connector. Used to unlock the S.T.A.R.S. Armory.

Gas Station Key

Gas Station — Opening Section

Found in the back storage area. Unlocks the exit door.

Car Key

Firing Range — Police Station B1

Found inside a tin box. Examine the key and press its button to unlock a police car boot in the Parking Garage, containing Claire's JMB Hp3 handgun.
