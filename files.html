Files and Documents

Throughout Resident Evil 2 Remake, you'll come across files, notes and documents scattered around Raccoon City. Some contain useful clues for solving puzzles and opening locked doors, while others reveal more about the outbreak and what happened to the people caught up in it. There are 58 files to collect, and some can only be found during Leon's or Claire's story. This guide will show you where to find every file, helping you collect them all and unlock the Lore Explorer achievement.
