Resident evil 2 remake guide

Welcome to my Resident Evil 2 Remake guide. I'll be covering both Leon and Claire's stories, including puzzle solutions, safe codes, locker combinations, weapon locations, hidden items and tips for dealing with some of the tougher enemies. Resident Evil 2 Remake is a perfect place to start if you're new to the Resident Evil series. You don't need to have played any of the previous games to enjoy it, and it's a great introduction to what Resident Evil is all about. With its mix of action, horror, exploration and puzzle-solving, there's plenty to keep you on your toes as you make your way through the zombie-infested streets of Raccoon City. Whether this is your first time playing or you're returning to complete everything you missed, this guide will help you find your way around, solve the trickier puzzles and track down useful items.

Leon and Claire's Campaigns

Leon Kennedy and Claire Redfield each have their own story, weapons and areas to explore. Although both characters visit many of the same locations, there are important differences between their campaigns. I'll be covering both characters separately, including their 2nd Run scenarios, so you can easily find the information you need for whichever character you're playing.

Puzzles, Safes and Locker Codes

Resident Evil 2 Remake is packed with puzzles, locked safes, lockers and hidden combinations. Some are straightforward, while others require finding clues scattered around the police station and other locations. I'll be including solutions for the statue medallion puzzles, electronic door panels, chess plug puzzle and greenhouse control room, along with all the safe combinations and locker codes.

Weapons, Upgrades and Hidden Items

Finding the right weapons and upgrades can make a huge difference, especially when ammunition is running low. I'll show you where to find the different weapons available to Leon and Claire, along with their upgrades, extra inventory space and other useful items. I'll also be covering the locations of Mr. Raccoon, files and other collectibles for anyone trying to complete everything the game has to offer.
