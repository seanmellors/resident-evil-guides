Mr Raccoon

Hidden throughout Resident Evil 2 Remake there is 15 Mr Raccoon toys waiting to be found. These small, colorful collectibles are easy to miss, especially when you're busy fighting zombies and trying to survive Raccoon City. Some can only be found while playing as Leon or Claire, and others require a 2nd Run. This guide will show you where to find every Mr Raccoon, so you can destroy all 15 and unlock the Complete Vermin Extermination** achievement.
