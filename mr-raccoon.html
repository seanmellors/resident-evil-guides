Mr. Raccoon locations

Resident Evil 2 Remake — All 15 collectible locations

Find all 15 Mr. Raccoon collectibles

Hidden throughout Resident Evil 2 Remake are 15 Mr. Raccoon toys waiting to be found. These small, colourful collectibles are easy to miss, especially when you're busy fighting zombies and trying to survive Raccoon City.

Some can only be found while playing as Leon or Claire, while another requires a 2nd Run. You'll need to explore both characters' campaigns to find them all.

Destroying all 15 unlocks the Complete Vermin Extermination achievement and the Infinite Combat Knife.

Once destroyed, a Mr. Raccoon stays counted across your playthroughs. You don't need to collect them all in one game.

Shared locations Leon only Claire only 2nd Run Tips

Shared locations

These nine Mr. Raccoons can be found across Leon and Claire's campaigns, although access to some areas depends on the character or scenario.

1. West Office

Location: Police Station — 1F

Character: Leon and Claire

Enter the West Office and look on top of the tall cabinet along the northern wall. The Mr. Raccoon is sitting among some cardboard boxes.

2. S.T.A.R.S. Office

Location: Police Station — 2F

Character: Leon and Claire

Inside the S.T.A.R.S. Office, head towards the southeastern corner of the room. Look behind the computer monitor on the desk to find the Mr. Raccoon.

3. Firing Range

Location: Police Station — B1

Character: Leon and Claire

Head to the Firing Range near the Parking Garage. Enter the shooting area and look along the floor beneath the targets. The Mr. Raccoon is sitting near one of the fallen targets.

4. East Hallway

Location: Police Station — 2F

Character: Leon and Claire

Head towards the staircase outside the Chief's Office, near the Art Room. The Mr. Raccoon is sitting on a small table beside the stairs.

5. Clock Tower hallway

Location: Police Station — 3F

Character: Leon and Claire

Leave the Clock Tower through the western door and follow the corridor towards the dead end. Look on the windowsill to find the Mr. Raccoon.

6. Break Room

Location: Police Station — 1F

Character: Leon, or Claire during the 2nd Run

Inside the Break Room, look beside the bunk beds. The Mr. Raccoon is hidden behind a bag near the foot of the bed.

7. Bottom Waterway

Location: Sewers — Lower Level

Character: Leon and Claire

Make your way through the Bottom Waterway towards the Supplies Storage Room. Before entering, look behind the table near the staircase to find the Mr. Raccoon.

8. Cafeteria

Location: NEST Laboratory — B1

Character: Leon and Claire

Inside the Cafeteria, head towards the ladder leading into the ventilation shaft. Look on the ledge near the ladder to find the Mr. Raccoon.

9. Nap Room

Location: NEST Laboratory — B1

Character: Leon and Claire

Return to the Nap Room after obtaining the Signal Modulator. Restore power to open the sleeping pods. The Mr. Raccoon is inside one of the pods.

Leon-only locations

These two Mr. Raccoons are exclusive to Leon's campaign. One is found during Ada's playable section.

10. Sewer Entrance

Location: Sewers

Character: Leon only

After escaping the giant crocodile, continue along the walkway towards the exit. Before climbing the ladder, look back towards the water. The Mr. Raccoon is sitting on a ledge nearby.

11. Incinerator

Location: Sewers — Incinerator

Character: Ada, during Leon's campaign

While controlling Ada, make your way towards the Incinerator. Before entering, look to the right of the entrance near the traffic cones. The Mr. Raccoon is hidden around the corner.

Claire-only locations

These three Mr. Raccoons can only be collected during Claire's campaign.

12. East Storage Room

Location: Police Station — 3F

Character: Claire only

Use the Heart Key to enter the small room connected to the East Storage Room. Look on the shelves near the doorway to find the Mr. Raccoon.

13. Bus

Location: Behind the Police Station

Character: Claire only

After leaving the Parking Garage, make your way through the basketball court towards the Orphanage. Enter the bus and look on the dashboard near the driver's seat.

14. Orphanage Nursery

Location: Orphanage — 2F

Character: Claire only

Head upstairs to the Nursery. The Mr. Raccoon is sitting on a shelf among the children's toys. You can spot it while playing as Sherry, but you'll need to return as Claire to destroy it.

2nd Run exclusive

This final Mr. Raccoon can only be found during a 2nd Run with either Leon or Claire.

15. Police Station entrance

Location: Police Station — 1F

Character: Leon or Claire

Scenario: 2nd Run only

At the beginning of the 2nd Run, head towards the side entrance of the Police Station. Look in the flowerbed beside the stairs to find the final Mr. Raccoon.

Mr. Raccoon tips

How to find and destroy Mr. Raccoons

Mr. Raccoons make a distinctive rattling sound when you're nearby. Listening carefully can help you locate them, especially when they're hidden behind objects.

You can destroy them using a gun or knife. Once destroyed, they remain counted across your playthroughs.

You must play both Leon and Claire's campaigns, including a 2nd Run, to find all 15.

Rewards

Vermin Extermination: Destroy your first Mr. Raccoon.

Complete Vermin Extermination: Destroy all 15 Mr. Raccoons.

Infinite Combat Knife: Unlocked after destroying all 15 Mr. Raccoons.
