Mr. Raccoon locations

Resident Evil 2 Remake — All 15 collectible locations

Find all 15 Mr. Raccoon collectibles

There are 15 Mr. Raccoon toys hidden throughout Resident Evil 2 Remake. You'll find them in places such as the Police Station, Sewers, Orphanage and NEST Laboratory. Some are easy to spot, while others are tucked away in places you might walk straight past.

Not every Mr. Raccoon is available to both characters. Some are exclusive to Leon, others can only be reached by Claire, and one is hidden in the 2nd Run scenario.

Destroying every Mr. Raccoon earns the Complete Vermin Extermination achievement and unlocks the Infinite Combat Knife.

Your progress carries over between playthroughs, so there's no need to destroy all 15 in a single run.

Shared locations Leon only Claire only 2nd Run Tips

Shared locations

These nine Mr. Raccoons are found in areas that can be visited across Leon and Claire's campaigns. Some routes and access requirements differ depending on your scenario.

1. West Office

Location: Police Station — 1F

Character: Leon and Claire

Go into the West Office and check the tall shelving along the northern side of the room. The toy is perched above the desks, partly surrounded by cardboard boxes.

2. S.T.A.R.S. Office

Location: Police Station — 2F

Character: Leon and Claire

Once you reach the S.T.A.R.S. Office, examine the desks towards the southeastern side of the room. The toy is tucked behind a computer monitor, making it difficult to notice unless you look around the desk.

3. Firing Range

Location: Police Station — B1

Character: Leon and Claire

Visit the Firing Range on the basement level and enter the shooting area. Check the floor around the targets and damaged equipment. You'll find the toy near one of the fallen targets.

4. Chief's Office staircase

Location: Police Station — 2F

Character: Leon and Claire

Make your way to the staircase on the eastern side of the Police Station, near the Chief's Office. Check the small table on the staircase landing for the Mr. Raccoon.

5. Clock Tower hallway

Location: Police Station — 3F

Character: Leon and Claire

After reaching the Clock Tower, explore the corridor that leads away from its western entrance. Follow the passage towards the end and inspect the windowsill. The Mr. Raccoon is sitting there.

6. Break Room

Location: Police Station — 1F

Character: Leon, or Claire during the 2nd Run

Enter the Break Room on the eastern side of the Police Station. Look around the bunk beds and the bags left beside them. The toy is hidden low down near the end of one of the beds.

7. Bottom Waterway

Location: Sewers — Lower Level

Character: Leon and Claire

Travel through the lower Sewers towards the Supplies Storage Room. Near the stairs leading to this area, search around the table and nearby objects. The Mr. Raccoon is concealed behind them.

8. Cafeteria

Location: NEST Laboratory — B1

Character: Leon and Claire

In the Laboratory Cafeteria, head towards the ladder that leads into the ventilation shaft. Before climbing up, turn your attention to the right-hand side. The Mr. Raccoon is resting on a ledge close to the ladder.

9. Nap Room

Location: NEST Laboratory — B1

Character: Leon and Claire

You'll need the Signal Modulator before you can collect this one. Return to the Nap Room and use the modulator to restore power. Once the sleeping pods open, check inside them to find the toy.

Leon-only locations

These two Mr. Raccoons are available during Leon's story. Make sure you collect the second one while controlling Ada.

10. Sewer Entrance

Location: Sewers

Character: Leon only

After Leon escapes from the giant crocodile, you'll reach a walkway with a ladder leading out of the area. Before leaving, turn around and look across the water. The Mr. Raccoon is positioned on a raised ledge on the opposite side.

11. Incinerator

Location: Sewers — Incinerator

Character: Ada, during Leon's campaign

During Ada's section, approach the Incinerator area and check the space to the right of its entrance. Look around the traffic cones and nearby objects. The toy is tucked into the corner.

Claire-only locations

These three collectibles are found along Claire's route, including the journey to the Orphanage.

12. East Storage Room

Location: Police Station — 3F

Character: Claire only

Use Claire's Heart Key to unlock the small room connected to the East Storage Room. Once inside, inspect the shelving close to the doorway. You'll find the Mr. Raccoon sitting among the stored items.

13. Bus

Location: Behind the Police Station

Character: Claire only

On your way to the Orphanage, you'll pass through the basketball court and reach a bus. Go inside rather than walking straight through the area. The Mr. Raccoon is on the dashboard near the driver's seat.

14. Orphanage Nursery

Location: Orphanage — 2F

Character: Claire only

Explore the upstairs Nursery in the Orphanage and look at the shelves holding children's toys. You'll notice a Mr. Raccoon among them. Although you can see it while controlling Sherry, you must return as Claire to destroy it.

2nd Run exclusive

The final collectible only appears during a 2nd Run. Either Leon or Claire can destroy it.

15. Police Station entrance

Location: Police Station — 1F

Character: Leon or Claire

Scenario: 2nd Run only

Near the beginning of your 2nd Run, approach the side entrance to the Police Station. Before going inside, search the flowerbed beside the stairs. The Mr. Raccoon is hidden among the plants.

Mr. Raccoon tips

How to find Mr. Raccoons

Listen for a rattling noise while exploring. Mr. Raccoons make a recognisable sound that can help you track them down, even when they're hidden behind furniture or other objects.

You can destroy a toy with either a weapon or your combat knife. Once you've destroyed one, it remains counted towards your total across future playthroughs.

You'll need to play through both characters' stories and start a 2nd Run to reach every collectible.

Rewards

Vermin Extermination: Destroy your first Mr. Raccoon.

Complete Vermin Extermination: Destroy all 15 Mr. Raccoons.

Infinite Combat Knife: Unlocked after destroying every Mr. Raccoon.
