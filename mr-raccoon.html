Resident Evil 2 Remake — All 15 collectible locations

Find all 15 Mr. Raccoon collectibles

Hidden throughout Resident Evil 2 Remake are 15 Mr. Raccoon toys waiting to be found. These small, colourful collectibles are easy to miss, especially when you're busy fighting zombies and trying to survive Raccoon City.

Some can only be found while playing as Leon or Claire, while another requires a 2nd Run. You'll need to explore both characters' campaigns to find them all.

This guide will show you where to find every Mr. Raccoon collectible, so you can destroy all 15 and unlock the Complete Vermin Extermination achievement.
