Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who runs this website

This independent Resident Evil 2 Remake fan guide is operated by Birdie & Sheep Web Development in the UK. For privacy enquiries or requests about your personal information, contact: birdieandsheep@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can read every guide without accepting analytics. We do not load Google Analytics or send analytics requests until you opt in. Accept and Reject have equal prominence. Choose to give or withdraw consent at any time. Closing the page or continuing to browse is not consent.

Storage on your device

  • Essential preference: local storage key re2-cookie-consent-v1 records your analytics choice, policy version and the time of your choice. It is used only to remember and apply your preference, without a visitor identifier. We ask again after 180 days. Browser storage may retain an expired record until it is replaced or cleared.
  • Optional Google Analytics cookies: _ga and _ga_H3CMPEQNXD distinguish visits and maintain session information. The site configures a maximum lifetime of 180 days without extending it on each visit; Google may use shorter session periods. These cookies are used only after consent.

Rejecting analytics also attempts to remove existing Google Analytics cookies accessible to this site. Withdrawing consent stops future collection and reloads the page to unload the analytics library. It does not automatically erase data previously sent to Google. Browser controls can also clear stored data.

Information collected with permission

Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-H3CMPEQNXD) helps us understand page visits, sessions, approximate location, device and browser information and interactions such as scrolling or outbound links, depending on the property's enhanced measurement settings. Google receives connection information including your IP address to provide its service. We do not deliberately send names, email addresses or account details. Do not put personal information in page URLs. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled by this site's configuration.

Our lawful basis for optional analytics processing is your consent under the UK GDPR; we also obtain permission for optional device storage under PECR. Essential preference storage is used to honour your requested privacy choice. Routine website delivery and security are supported by our legitimate interests in operating a secure, accessible guide.

Hosting and other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request and security information, including IP addresses, under its own policies. The site loads the existing Bebas Neue and Roboto fonts from Google Fonts; those requests disclose connection information to Google even when analytics is rejected. Fonts are separate from analytics and are not used here to set analytics cookies. Links to other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. There are no account, checkout or contact-form features in this version of the guide.

Recipients, overseas processing and retention

GitHub and Google process relevant information in accordance with their service terms and privacy notices. Data may be processed outside the UK. Their published terms describe applicable safeguards, including contractual protections and recognised transfer frameworks where applicable. See GitHub's privacy statement, Google's privacy policy and Google Fonts privacy information.

The preference lasts 180 days as described above. Analytics event and user data retention is controlled separately in the Google Analytics account; aggregated reports can be retained longer. Hosting logs follow GitHub's retention practices. Contact us for the current account retention setting or to request deletion of identifiable data where possible.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal information, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need enough information to identify the relevant data and cannot necessarily identify an individual from aggregate reports. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority.

Changes

We will update this page if our services or use of information change. Material changes to analytics purposes will require a new consent choice.
